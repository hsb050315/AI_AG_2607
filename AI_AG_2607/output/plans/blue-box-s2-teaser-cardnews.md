# Blue Box Season 2 Teaser — Card News Information Structure Plan

- Subject: Blue Box (アオのハコ) Season 2 teaser promotion — real Netflix anime, do not fabricate plot facts
- Deliverable: 6 sections. Section 1 = thumbnail. Sections 2–6 = a 기승전결 (setup / development / turn / resolution) narrative arc
- Design concept (source of truth = Phase-1 design-concept doc "푸른 상자 2기 티저"):
  - Theme: "a youth gymnasium sunk blue like underwater"
  - Dominant #1C3F73 (deep blue) + Secondary #4FA3C7 (aqua)
  - Accent #FF7A5C (dawn coral) — "a single strand of early-morning light", ~10% usage only
  - Mood: plain and restrained negative space, the stillness of winter, slow-burn romance + sports
  - Type/layout: bold sans-serif headline + plain body, teaser / card-news layout
- Reference roles:
  - `cn_01` (한국저작권위원회 AI 저작권 card news): frame-to-frame rhythm — hook → explanation → conclusion → contrast/split → typographic frame → CTA; keyword highlight boxes; consistent per-frame motif
  - `cn_02` (서울시 협동조합 9단계 card news): **structure/layout only, color NOT used** — cover → staged content → closing-contact sequencing, numbered step labels, stable header position

## Global rules

- Canvas 1080×1080 (square), safe margin 96px all sides
- Palette: background #1C3F73; aqua #4FA3C7 for sub-elements, lines, tags; coral #FF7A5C only for one accent per frame (a word, an underline, a dot) and never above ~10% area
- Text: near-white #F2F6FA body on deep blue; headline weight ExtraBold/Black, body weight Regular
- Every frame carries the same quiet header: small "青のbox / BLUE BOX" wordmark top-left, frame number "02 / 06" top-right in aqua
- One idea per frame. Wide top negative space. No more than ~25 Korean characters per headline line
- Water motif: faint caustic-light gradient or a thin horizontal "surface line" that sits a little lower each frame (2→6), as if the viewer is sinking — visual through-line for the arc
- Every factual claim (broadcast timing, platform, episode info, staff, cast, quotes) is a PLACEHOLDER here and must be replaced with confirmed official information before publishing

## Section 1 — Thumbnail (feed cover)

- Purpose: stop the scroll; instantly say "Blue Box is coming back" and set the underwater-gym mood
- Elements:
  - Full-bleed key visual area (official S2 teaser still / key art — placeholder), deep-blue graded so it reads at feed thumbnail size
  - Headline: "푸른 상자" locked large, with "2기" set in coral as the single accent
  - Support line (small, aqua): "티저 공개" / short teaser tagline — placeholder for official copy
  - Surface line sits near the very top (the descent begins here)
  - No body text, no logos clutter — cover must survive being shown at 150px
- Layout ref: `cn_02` cover framing (big title, calm field); accent discipline from concept

## Section 2 — 기 (Setup): return to that blue gym

- Purpose: re-establish the world and the feeling season 1 left behind; welcome lapsed viewers
- Elements:
  - Headline: "그 파란 체육관으로 돌아온다" (placeholder wording)
  - Body 2–3 lines: one-sentence reminder of the premise at concept level — a sports academy, early-morning practice, a quiet crush living under the same roof. Keep it emotional, not a plot recap, and free of specifics that need sourcing
  - Visual: wide empty gym floor, light from high windows, single figure small in frame — maximum negative space
  - Surface line ~1/4 down
- Ref: `cn_01` opening "hook" frame rhythm

## Section 3 — 승 (Development): the feelings that season 1 built

- Purpose: raise the emotional stakes — why viewers cared
- Elements:
  - Headline: "한 지붕 아래, 말하지 못한 마음" (placeholder)
  - Body: 2–3 short lines on the slow-burn core (proximity, restraint, practice-court routine) — character dynamics described at a level that does not assert unconfirmed S2 events
  - Optional: 2 small aqua keyword chips (e.g. "슬로우번", "청춘 스포츠") — `cn_01` highlight-box device
  - Visual: two figures sharing a frame but not looking at each other; coral accent on a single small object between them
  - Surface line ~1/2 down
- Ref: `cn_01` "explanation" frame; `cn_02` staged-label device (chips instead of Step.0X)

## Section 4 — 전 (Turn): what season 2 changes

- Purpose: the pivot — signal that something shifts, without spoilers
- Elements:
  - Headline: "이제, 계절이 바뀐다" (placeholder — pick a turn line that is true of any new season: a new term, a new competition, a new tension)
  - Body: 1–2 lines, deliberately open. Frame it as a question the teaser poses, not an answer
  - Visual: strongest contrast frame — split composition (like `cn_01`'s VS frame) or a hard diagonal; coral used on the dividing line
  - Surface line ~3/4 down (viewer is deep now)
- Ref: `cn_01` split / "VS" contrast frame

## Section 5 — 전 심화 (Turn, intensified): the teaser's one decisive image

- Purpose: the peak — give one concrete, quotable hook from the actual teaser
- Elements:
  - Headline: a short line or on-screen quote pulled verbatim from the official teaser (placeholder — must be a real line from the released teaser, quoted exactly)
  - Body: minimal or none — let the line carry it
  - Visual: the single most striking teaser frame (placeholder still), near-full-bleed, deep blue crush; coral accent as a thin underline under the quote only
  - Surface line at the very bottom edge — fully submerged
- Ref: `cn_01` large-typography frame (the "AI" frame equivalent)

## Section 6 — 결 (Resolution): when and where to watch

- Purpose: convert attention into a plan to watch; the only information-dense frame
- Elements:
  - Headline: "푸른 상자 2기" + release window (placeholder — official date/quarter)
  - Info block (aqua labels, white values), each a placeholder until officially confirmed:
    - 공개: [official date]
    - 플랫폼: Netflix [confirm exclusivity/wording]
    - 그 외: [episode count / staff / cast — only if officially announced]
  - CTA line in coral: "지금, 티저 확인" + where (official channel handle — placeholder)
  - Visual: calmest frame — surface line back at the top, light returning; mirrors Section 1 so the set closes where it opened
  - Layout ref: `cn_02` closing-contact frame (structure only, not its bright palette)
